# Evaluating structure discovery and explanations

The harness in `tests/eval/` scores what the app already does (phrase descriptions, clustering,
maps) against known annotations. It runs natively on the same Python modules as the Pyodide
worker, with the app's default settings, and keeps all data local.

## Data: Rechtspraak sample

`npm run prepare:rechtspraak` samples Dutch court rulings from the CC0 open-data API
(`data.rechtspraak.nl`) into the ignored `fixtures/rechtspraak-sample.json`. Raw responses are
cached in `.cache/rechtspraak/`, so reruns are offline. The sample is balanced on purpose:
10 rulings for each of 12 legal sub-areas and each year 2016-2025 (1,190 after filtering; the download was stopped early, so this is smaller than the first plan of 3,000), drawn at uniformly random
offsets with a fixed seed, keeping only rulings that carry exactly one legal area. Balanced
quotas give a known chance level; they do not represent the real mix of rulings.

Columns: legal area and sub-area (two-level gold hierarchy), court, court type, procedure, year,
ruling date, two text fields (`summary`, `ruling_excerpt`), two length counts and the ECLI
running number.

The API rejects the default Python User-Agent, so the script sends a generic client name.

## Gold manifest

`tests/eval/rechtspraak-gold.json` states, per column, whether the text is expected to vary with
it (strong, moderate, weak, length, uncertain) and why. It was written before the first
evaluation run. Null controls are added by the harness: shuffled copies of real columns and
random noise columns.

## What is measured

1. **Which columns does the text vary with?** Each column is scored by how strongly phrase usage
   differs across its groups, calibrated against label shuffles. Two variants are compared: a
   naive one and one that works inside text-length strata. Graded by whether strong columns
   outrank every null control, AUC against the nulls, the false-positive rate on null controls,
   and whether length columns rank below the strong ones. This screen is a candidate; the app
   has no equivalent yet.
2. **Do phrase descriptions explain a group?** For each group (12 sub-areas, 3 areas) against
   the rest, the app's `describe_terms` ranks phrases on a random half of the records. The top
   10 phrases are judged on the other half: AUC for recovering membership, mean phrase
   precision, member coverage. Random groups of matching sizes give the chance level, and a
   longest-quartile group probes the length confound. Averaged over three random splits.
3. **Do clusters and maps recover the known structure?** The app's text features (TF-IDF, SVD)
   feed its MiniBatch K-means, agglomerative and HDBSCAN. Agreement is ARI and NMI against area,
   sub-area and other columns, with shuffled labels as chance. Maps (PCA, SVD, UMAP with the
   app's defaults) are scored by the share of each record's 10 nearest neighbours that share
   its label, against a shuffled-label baseline.

Both text fields are run. The summary is short (median 27 words) and sometimes names the legal
area. The excerpt is 2,500 characters of header and opening text. I expected the excerpt to leak
less; the first run does not support that (it recovers the labels better, see below).

## Results of the first run

Full tables: [evaluation-rechtspraak-report.md](evaluation-rechtspraak-report.md), regenerated by
`npm run eval:rechtspraak`. One sample, one seed, three random splits; no significance testing
beyond the paired comparison noted below.

- **Controls behave.** Random groups score AUC 0.46-0.54 for every ranking, shuffled and noise
  columns never outrank a strong column, and shuffled labels give ARI of 0.00.
- **Descriptions.** `contrast` (the app's Automatic choice when a comparison exists) beats the
  other rankings: on `summary`, mean held-out AUC 0.78 on sub-areas against 0.70-0.73; on
  `ruling_excerpt`, 0.96 against 0.80-0.93. Per group it wins 11-15 of 15 comparisons (the group
  bootstrap interval is optimistic because sub-areas and areas overlap). "Balanced splits" is the
  weakest, which fits that it never looks at the comparison.
- **Clusters.** Recovery depends on the text: on `summary` (median 27 words) MiniBatch reaches
  ARI 0.16 on sub-areas, on `ruling_excerpt` 0.57. HDBSCAN leaves 54% and 18% unassigned.
- **Maps.** UMAP keeps nearly all of the 32-d neighbourhood label purity (0.84 vs 0.84 on fine
  areas for the excerpt); PCA and SVD keep the coarse level but lose most of the fine level
  (0.46 and 0.42).
- **Column screen.** Passes its primary grade (every strong column above every null control), but
  the manifest was wrong in three places and the screen has a calibration problem, below.

### Where the first run disagrees with the gold manifest

The manifest was not edited after seeing results. Its misses:

- On `ruling_excerpt`, `year`, `court` and `court_type` rank above the strong sub-area column.
  The excerpt starts with the court name, date and case number, so these columns are echoed in the
  text. "Weak" was a wrong expectation for that field.
- `ruling_words` was labelled a pure length confound. The length control only removes the length
  of the analysed field, so for `summary` it is a real property of the ruling (criminal
  judgments run longer) and ranks third. The `length_below_all_strong` grade fails on the excerpt
  for this reason, and is not evidence about the screen.
- The length-controlled screen splits records into strata, which costs power for columns with
  many groups: `legal_subarea` (12 groups) falls from z 618 to 42 on the excerpt while
  `legal_area` (3 groups) keeps 598. z is not comparable across columns with different group
  counts, and one of 21 null columns reached z 3.4, so a fixed threshold of z > 3 is not
  calibrated. Rank against the null controls instead of thresholding.
- On Rechtspraak the naive screen was not misled by length (it passes every grade); the length
  confound that motivated the control was seen on DBRD only.

## Controls on the harness

`npm run test:eval` runs `tests/eval/check_harness.py` on synthetic data with planted structure:
the length-controlled screen must find planted columns and ignore noise, and descriptions must
recover planted groups but not random ones.

## Known weaknesses of the evaluation

- Gold labels are procedural as well as topical: party roles (verdachte, gedaagde, appellant)
  track the legal area, so high recovery partly measures register, not topic.
- Phrase quality is only read off examples: single digits and letters ("1", "2", "c") appear as
  distinctive phrases in excerpts, and nested n-grams (voet, staande voet) survive deduplication.
  Neither is quantified yet.
- 1,190 rulings; one seed.

## Not covered

- Embedding-based maps and clusters (sentence or word vectors): the model assets are not in a
  plain checkout and the semantic ranking needs saved embeddings.
- Human tasks: time, steps and correctness for real users, and the simulated analyst.
- Other datasets and dtype mixes.
