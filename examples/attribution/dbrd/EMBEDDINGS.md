# Sentence embeddings of the DBRD sample

`dbrd-test.text.dutch-minilm.npy` (a `data-v1` release asset, restored when the site is built) holds one
half-precision vector per review of `dbrd-test.json`, made with MarkSelles/dutch-minilm-l6-v2 (Apache-2.0).
The vectors are derived from the reviews, so they carry the dataset's licence, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (see `LICENSE`):
attribute Benjamin van der Burgh and Suzan Verberne, use them non-commercially and share them alike.
